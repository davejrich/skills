## Fictional transcript

**Session:** 8 June 2026, Australia/Brisbane. Participants: Coach and Team Lead. Operations Manager is absent.

- 00:10 Team Lead: I want fewer routine questions coming to me so I can spend more time planning. I haven't measured the interruptions yet.
- 00:35 Coach: You could try delegating five recurring tasks this week.
- 00:50 Team Lead: Five is too many. I'll draft a checklist for one recurring task by Friday 12 June.
- 01:15 Coach: I'll send you a checklist template tomorrow.
- 01:30 Team Lead: Actually, Monday 15 June is more realistic for my draft. That's the date I'll commit to.
- 01:45 Coach: Monday 15 June it is. What will be in the draft?
- 02:00 Team Lead: The steps, the decisions the team can make, and when they should come back to me.
- 02:20 Coach: Could the Operations Manager approve it?
- 02:30 Team Lead: Possibly. I need to check who has approval authority first. I'll ask about that, but I can't promise approval.
- 02:50 Team Lead: I've already shared the current task instructions with you in the chat.
- 03:00 Coach: Thanks, I can see them. I'll read them before our next session.
- 03:20 Coach: Shall we review your draft on Tuesday 16 June at 09:00 Brisbane time?
- 03:30 Team Lead: Yes. I'll send the calendar invite today.
- 03:45 Coach: Agreed. We'll review the draft then. We can discuss measuring interruptions at that meeting; let's not set a target today.
