# Summary and evidence format

## Client-facing draft

**Draft for coach review**

**Session:** title or focus, date if known  
**Participants:** confirmed names/roles only

### Session at a glance

Three to five sentences on the focus, key insights and agreed direction. Avoid reproducing the meeting chronologically.

### Progress and insights

Record relevant progress, learning and challenges in the client's terms. Distinguish self-reported progress from a verified result. Omit this section if nothing substantive was discussed.

### Goals and success measures

| Goal / desired outcome | Success measure or evidence | Target / review date | Agreement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source-supported goal | Supplied measure, or Not defined | Supplied date, or Not agreed | Agreed / Proposed / To confirm |

### Decisions and agreed accountabilities

Summarise significant decisions, then list only accepted actions:

| Action | Accountable owner | Due / review | Completion evidence | Support / dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specific source-supported commitment | Confirmed owner | Confirmed date, or Not agreed | Agreed evidence, or Not defined | Supplied support, or None recorded |

Use separate rows for independently owned work. A recurring action may include a source-supported frequency; do not invent one. If no agreed actions exist, state that instead of padding the table.

### Points to confirm

List proposals, unclear assignments, missing dates/measurements and any decisions awaiting confirmation. Phrase each as a concise question, with the relevant context. Do not restate them as commitments.

### Before the next session

Briefly recap the agreed priorities and next check-in, if established. No new homework. If no next session was set, say "Next session: not agreed in the source."

## Separate coach review — not part of the client copy

### Evidence ledger

| ID | Goal / decision / action / progress / blocker | Source wording or faithful excerpt | Speaker + locator | Status | Owner | Date wording → resolved date | Gaps / superseded item |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use concise excerpts needed to substantiate each item. Include a source reference for sensitive omissions only where necessary; avoid copying unrelated disclosures into the ledger.

### Review checks

- Source type and coverage, including missing sections or unclear speakers.
- Owners, dates and measures traced to source evidence.
- Suggestions separated from accepted commitments.
- Later changes and unresolved contradictions accounted for.
- Audience and sensitive omissions appropriate.
- Questions requiring coach/client confirmation before release.
