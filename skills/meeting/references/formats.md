# Agenda and minutes formats

## Agenda

```
# Agenda: {Meeting title}
{Day DD Mon YYYY}, {start}-{end} {time zone} · {conference link}
Attendees: {names}

## Outcome we want
{One or two sentences: what has to be true when the meeting ends}

## Decisions needed
1. {decision}
2. ...

## Agenda
| Time | Item | Lead | Goal |
|------|------|------|------|
| {start} | Check-in | {user} | |
| ... | {item} | {name} | Decide / Inform / Discuss |
| {end - 5 min} | Recap actions and next step | {user} | Owners and dates agreed |

## Carried over
{Open actions and questions from previous minutes or email, or "Nothing carried over"}

## Background (user only)
{Short notes from email, tasks or project notes the user should have in mind. Remove this section from any copy that goes to attendees}
```

Use clock times for a shared agenda and elapsed minutes ("0-5 min") for a private one, whichever the user prefers.

## Minutes

```
# Minutes: {Meeting title}
{Day DD Mon YYYY}, {start}-{end} {time zone}
Attendees: {names} · Absent: {names, if any}
Source: {verbatim transcript / AI notes / user's notes}

## Summary
{Three or four sentences: what the meeting was for and where it landed}

## Decisions
1. {decision}: {who agreed, any conditions}

## Action items
| # | Action | Owner | Due |
|---|--------|-------|-----|
| 1 | {verb-first action} | {confirmed owner} | {YYYY-MM-DD, or TBC} |

## Agenda coverage
| Agenda item | Status | Notes |
|-------------|--------|-------|
| {item} | Covered / Partial / Not covered | |

## Open questions
{Raised but not resolved, unclear owners or dates, or "None"}

## Next meeting
{Date and time if agreed, or "Not set"}
```

Leave out Agenda coverage when no agenda was found, and say so in chat.

## Checks before saving

- Every owner and due date traces to something said in the meeting.
- Suggestions sit in Open questions, not Action items.
- Later changes override earlier statements.
- Every agenda item has a coverage status.
- Background notes do not appear in anything attendees will see.
